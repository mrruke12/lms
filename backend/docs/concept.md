The idea of the Learning Management System was motivated as a partial replacement of the manual assessment during the educational process. In other words, it's not supposed to completely substitute lessons, lectures, practice, etc, but reduce the load on the teacher by providing the following tools:
1. Lessons constructor;
2. Automatic grading of assignments that do not require direct teacher involvement;
3. An opportunity to manually grade specific tasks and sum up the grade with the automatic one;
4. Monitor academic progress;
5. Sync the grades to Platonus (\*requires platonus API).
